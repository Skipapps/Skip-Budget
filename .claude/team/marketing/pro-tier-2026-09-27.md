# Free vs Pro: team consensus, 2026-09-27

All 14 members polled against the same fact sheet. CEO synthesis below; no code changed.

## Consensus
| Question | Team view | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Dark mode | Free (accents stay Pro) | 13 of 14 for free dark mode; Tara, Tia, Max would free accents too |
| Loan calculator | One-off quote free, saving/schedule/overpayment Pro | 10 of 14 |
| Insights | This month (or a Home card / blurred preview) free, history and periods Pro | 7 of 14 |
| Free limits | Keep 1 card/account/income | 7; raise to 2: Marcus, Dilip; 3/3/2: Priya |
| Scanning | Pro | 13; Marcus: 5 free scans a month |
| Splits | Pro to create; free friends may join a Pro member's group | Diego, Dmitri |
| Price | Keep $1.99/mo, $19.99/yr | 13; Max: yearly $24.99 |
| Trial | 7 days, yearly only | 10; Max: 14 days |
| Badge | "Save 16%" computed from StoreKit, no hardcoded USD | unanimous |
| Conversion | Paywall at the moment of need, never generic | unanimous |

## Bugs and gaps found during the poll
1. Lapsed Pro members cannot record settlements or accept friend requests, so they cannot pay back what they owe (Diego).
2. The paywall can show the yearly trial on the monthly plan; eligibility is not checked (Dilip, pro.tsx:207).
3. The RevenueCat webhook skips TRANSFER events, so a restore onto another account leaves the server wall locked (Dilip).
4. Nothing reads WALL/FREE_LIMITS in src/lib/wall.ts; locks are hardcoded in 19 files plus SQL (Dmitri).
5. Scan/Upload on add-receipt carry no PRO badge; the lock appears only after the tap (Dana).
6. Paywall shows "/yr/yr" when the store returns no products (Priya).
7. Webhook stores only pro + expiry, not trial or billing-grace state (Diego).
8. Saved loans are walled only in the app; the database has no check (Diego).
9. No test proves client and server walls agree (Theo).
10. App Store Connect / RevenueCat prerequisites unverified: Paid Apps Agreement, banking, tax, pricing & availability, subscriptions Ready to Submit, entitlement, current offering, server notification URL (Dilip).
