Spending habits: new cards + View details

Mock for your review. It is set on Friday, Oct 16 so the week is part-way through. Every number comes from the rules below; nothing is typed in by hand.

You can tap things: the week arrows, any empty or filled day circle, and the weeks on View details (each one opens to show its days).

  • Spend limitPrice × the days the habit tracks that week: $5 × 7 = $35. Breakfast started on Wednesday, so its first week is $4 × 5 = $20.
  • SavedSame rule as now: each finished day without a receipt saves one price. Today only counts once it's over. When a week ends, Spent + Saved = the limit.
  • Day N of 7Weeks still run Monday to Sunday. Friday is "Day 5 of 7"; Breakfast is "Day 3 of 5". A finished week says "Week complete".
  • ButtonsThe pencil opens the edit page (Delete is there). View details opens the habit's history. The card itself doesn't open anything, so a tap meant for a day can't open a page by mistake.
